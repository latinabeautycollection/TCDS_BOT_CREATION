# Domain boundary
- ZSL1 owns external tax source identity, retrieval, release, raw evidence, source precedence, reconciliation, certification, and national publication.
- ZSL2 owns canonical geographic identity. ZSL1/ZSL5 may reference/bind but do not mutate ZSL2 geography.
- ZSL5 owns tax determination and tax/government-acquisition-cost evidence. It does not own BUY/NO-BUY, ROI, max bid, retailer search targets, scraper billing, purchase readiness, or checkout.
- R1A remains `retail.effective_search_targets`; R1F remains provider financial consumption.
- ARB integration is separate and must consume sealed ZSL5 quote evidence rather than maintaining a competing flat tax authority.
